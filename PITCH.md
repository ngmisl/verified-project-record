# Verified Project Record — pitch

## Problem

Off-plan buyers in Dubai pay in stages against construction progress, but
the proof of that progress is scattered: site reports, procurement
records, certificates, sales documents. When a payment depends on it, the
information exists but is fragmented across teams and systems, and is hard
to find or prove. Developers lose time and money; buyers lose trust.

DLD already publishes a certified completion percentage and escrow moves
only against certified milestones. What nobody has is the evidence between
certifications: the documents behind the number, in one place, checkable
by the people carrying the risk.

## Solution

Aunova Trust Infrastructure turns the scattered information a real estate
development produces into one trusted project record: structured,
permissioned, source-backed, and AI-ready.

- Every entry is extracted from a source document and verified field by
  field against its source line.
- Entries are hash-chained and signed, so any edit is caught and named.
- The head hash is timestamped, so the record is provable months later.
- Teams and buyers ask the project questions and get answers scoped to
  their access rights, each traceable to its source, instead of taking
  marketing claims on trust.

It sits across the tools developers already use (Procore, ERP) as a trust
layer. It replaces none of them.

## Why now

- Dubai off-plan sales run at record volume, and payment schedules are
  tied to construction milestones.
- AI document extraction is now good enough to structure the record, and
  cheap enough per document to run on every project.
- Buyers, lenders, and investors all want the same thing: proof, not
  promises. The record serves all three from one source.

## Business model

Per-project subscriptions for developers (the record is built as the
project runs), plus verification seats for the people who rely on it:
lenders checking before releasing money, investors watching a portfolio,
buyers verifying before paying an instalment. Pricing to be confirmed in
pilot talks; the demo carries no prices.
